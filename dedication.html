Dedication

To George G. Neville Jr.

This space is for a few words from his son.

This site continues the one that George G. Neville Jr. built and kept for the Alpha Recon Association for more than twenty years. Every account, name, date and photograph here was gathered, checked and published by him. What follows is taken from his own pages.

  • He served in Alpha Company, 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion, and is on the roster as George G. Neville, Jr., 1966. (Those That Served)
  • In August 1989, after 22 years, he was one of six men from the company who found each other and met in Rockville, Maryland: Ray Strohl, Al Rooney, Bob Young, Gerry Anderson, Bob Bruder and George Neville. That meeting began the association. (History of the 3rd Recon Association)
  • He was elected the first President of the Alpha Recon Association at its first meeting, on April 28, 1990 in Las Vegas, and served from 1990 to 1998. (Constitution and By-Laws, Company Office)
  • He was the association's webpage developer and its liaison for the men still missing in action. (Company Office)
  • He compiled the battalion's order of battle from the command chronologies. (3rd Reconnaissance Battalion Order of Battle)
  • On 11 November 1992 he placed a wreath at the Vietnam Memorial Wall with Fred Murray and Tom Boland. (In Our Memory)
  • He wrote the tributes to his friends on this site, among them the one to Gerald Anderson, signed "My Brother - My Best Friend". (Gerald H. Anderson)

Never Without Heroes

Lawrence C. Vetter Jr., who served with the battalion in 1966 and was a charter member of the association, wrote its history: Never Without Heroes: Marine Third Reconnaissance Battalion in Vietnam, 1965-70 (Ivy Books, 1996). In his Acknowledgments he wrote:

I would like to repeat here my sincere gratitude to Floyd Nagler. And there is one individual who must be mentioned again and his name underscored, for without him in particular there would be no present book: George Neville.

and of the association George helped to found:

If those who put together the 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion Association had not done so, this story would have been very limited in scope.

George listed the book in his own recommended reading. (George's Recommended Reading)
