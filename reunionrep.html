Reunion Report New Orleans 2000

From an earlier version of the association's website, saved 9 November 2004.

Officers – Contact Information

By-Laws - Alpha Recon Association

I would like to begin in expressing my extreme gratitude to Ray Raymond and Bill Muter (Delta Company) for their tireless efforts that went into the planning, coordination, and especially the personal sacrifice of their time, in pulling off a very wonderful reunion. This reunion meant so much to so many. The Brotherhood that flowed between those that attended is impossible to describe. It can only be experienced. I can not begin to describe those feelings so I won’t even try.

Over 250 men and their families attended New Orleans Y2K. Alpha Company had the largest turnout with over 70 members attending.

During the reunion, the activities were well planned and coordinated. Each day brought even more excitement then the previous. Our members took part in the Riverboat Luncheon, Sunrise Ceremony, the 3rd Recon Veterans Day Parade to the D-Day Museum, Museum Tour, Reunion Banquet, Marine Corps Birthday Ball, and many other impromptu gatherings of men and their families. Most attendees also experienced for the first time the many activities presented by the New Orleans Community such as the famed French Quarter and the many excellent restaurants.

The guest of honor at the Banquet was Col. Howard Lovingood who gave a very moving tribute to the 3rd Recon Association. The Colonel also passed on greetings and appreciation for our efforts and sacrifices made by General Jones, Commandant USMC. Col. Lovingood described how our legacy is very long and honored and how well known our association has become in Marine Circles today.

The "Colonel" holds the standard of dedication to God, Corps, and Country by serving forty-four years, five months and 25 days of active duty service. The Colonel served in Alpha Company during 1966 and 1967 as Company Gunny. We all were very proud of the "Colonel"

The Association was also honored at the Marine Corps Ball and the New Orleans Marines from the 4th Marine Division wore out our arms in handshakes. Those Marines never missed an opportunity to welcome us to New Orleans and express their thanks for our service to our country.

On 9 November Alpha Company held a company meeting in its hotel suite which became our Harborsite during the reunion. During the meeting many topics were discussed and voted on. It was a consensus opinion that many have wanted to attend a reunion in Washington DC. For many reasons that location has never been a reunion location. Further information was discussed on why many have not attended a reunion recently and the main reason was that some men only want to attend an Alpha Company gathering, feeling the Battalion Reunions are just too large. Alpha Company has not held a company reunion since 1992. A motion was made to hold an Alpha Company Reunion in November 2002 in conjunction with the 20th Anniversary of The Wall and the Marine Corps Birthday. The motion was seconded and passed without a single dissenting vote. Ray Raymond will again will be our point man and since the reunion, plans have been set in place and Alpha Company will hold its next formation in Washington DC

Two additional business items were discussed and voted on by the membership. It was brought up for discussion that the term of office for our officers needed to be amended to facilitate regular company elections. It was proposed that the term of office for our executive committee run from reunion to reunion. A by-laws change was proposed and passed unanimously. Some additional housekeeping was accomplished after a proposal was made to remove the wording in the membership section of our by-laws to remove the requirement of an initial fifteen-dollar donation. This was also passed without a dissenting vote.

Elections were held for the 2000 Executive Board and the names and contact information for each is linked at the top of this page. Our By-laws are also contained here also.

I was very pleased to see all the activity and interest re-kindled again at the company level. As I was giving my report, I looked out over the members gathered in the room, I was truly moved by the interest, care, and the concern, I saw on the faces of all the Brothers there. There was absolutely no dissension, everyone was pulling together on the same rope, and all wanted what was best for Alpha Company. It was one of the greatest meetings I’ve ever had the privilege to preside over.

We also discussed the need to publish a regular company newsletter and we will send one out shortly and follow up with newsletters every six months.

I am very pleased to again represent Alpha Company as your Company President and look forward to working with a very dedicated and motivated executive board.

Please contact me regarding any ideas you may have. Also let’s get into the habit of calling a Recon Brother once a month.

Thank You for your support –Thank you for your vote of confidence!!!

Semper Fidelis,

Jeff "Striker" Savelkoul

Alpha Recon Association. Alpha Company, 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion, United States Marine Corps, Vietnam 1965 to 1969.
