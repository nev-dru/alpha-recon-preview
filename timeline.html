Alpha Company timeline

Alpha Company, 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion, in Vietnam from May 1965 to October 1969. This timeline covers Alpha Company only; the battalion's other companies are not shown, apart from where they were based.

Battalion movements (all companies)

  • 07 May 1965 Battalion Lands at Red Beach One – Chu Lai Vietnam
  • 23 May 1965 Battalion displaced from Chu Lai and moved to Danang by ship LST Westchester County. Company B remains at Chu Lai
  • 27 May 1965 Company C displaced to Phu Bai TOAR; Company A, D, H&S at Danang TAOR; Company B at Chu Lai (Recon Group Alpha)
  • 13 Sep 1965 Company C (-) 1st & 2nd Plt’s moved from Phu Bai to Camp Reasoner Danang. Joins Company A , D, H&S . 3rd Plt remains at Phu Bai.
  • 19 Sep 1965 Company C 1st Recon Battalion OP Con to 3rd Recon Bn and is at Chu Lai TOAR ( Recon Group Alpha )
  • 1 Nov 1965 1st Force Reconnaissance Company placed under OP CON to 3rd Recon Bn.
  • 30 Nov 1965 Danang - Companies A,D,C(-), H&S,; 1st Force - Camp Merrill Danang.; Chu Lai - Company B 3rd Recon Bn & Company C 1st Recon at Chu Lai, (Recon Group Alpha )
  • 28 Feb 1966 Danang - Company A, C(-), D, H&S Camp Reasoner; Chu Lai - Company B 3rd Recon Bn., Company C, A, 1st Recon Bn Recon Group Alpha
