    Show the other 5We also gather to pay tribute to one of our living veterans, Bob Lake from Aitkin, Minnesota to present him with an award that he so justly deserves from a time so long ago.Bob Lake was a member of recon team American Beauty. In early 1969 his team was operating out of northern I corps running patrols in and out of the DMZ. Gathering information on enemy troop buildups in the area. A not so friendly place for a young kid from Aitkin to be.Jeff Savelkoul then read the citation and saluted Bob LakeCitation for Robert W. Lake's Purple Heart AwardRobert W. Lake, of Aitkin, MN, during June 1969, was part of a six man Marine Corps reconnaissance patrol that had been inserted into the DMZ June 6 to report on a North Vietnamese Army Battalion.
