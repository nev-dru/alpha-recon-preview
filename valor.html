This page contains the names of the men who served in Alpha Company 3rd Recon Battalion who received the Medal Of Honor, Navy Cross, Silver Star, Bronze Star w Combat "V" device and Navy Commendation w "V" device. The listings for the MOH and Navy Cross are complete. If you received an award while serving with Alpha Company and your name is not listed here please contact George Neville .
