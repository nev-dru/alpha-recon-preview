3rd Reconnaissance Battalion Order of Battle

3rd Reconnaissance Battalion Order of Battle –Vietnam War

By: George G. Neville, Jr.

Source: Command Chronology - 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion, 3rd Marine Division
07 May 1965 Battalion Lands at Red Beach One – Chu Lai Vietnam
23 May 1965 Battalion displaced from Chu Lai and moved to Danang by ship LST Westchester County. Company B remains at Chu Lai
27 May 1965 Company C displaced to Phu Bai TOAR
Company A, D, H&S at Danang TAOR
Company B at Chu Lai (Recon Group Alpha)
13 Sep 1965 Company C (-) 1st & 2nd Plt’s moved from Phu Bai to Camp Reasoner Danang. Joins Company A , D, H&S . 3rd Plt remains at Phu Bai.
19 Sep 1965 Company C 1st Recon Battalion OP Con to 3rd Recon Bn and is at Chu Lai TOAR ( Recon Group Alpha )
1 Nov 1965 1st Force Reconnaissance Company placed under OP CON to 3rd Recon Bn.
30 Nov 1965 Danang - Companies A,D,C(-), H&S,
1st Force - Camp Merrill Danang.
Chu Lai - Company B 3rd Recon Bn & Company C 1st Recon at Chu Lai, (Recon Group Alpha )
28 Feb 1966 Danang - Company A, C(-), D, H&S Camp Reasoner
Chu Lai - Company B 3rd Recon Bn., Company C, A, 1st Recon Bn Recon Group Alpha
19 Mar 1966 Phu Bai - Company B detached Chu Lai and moved to Phu Bai OP CON 4th Marines
24 Mar 1966 Phu Bai - Company D moved to - 4th Marines (Recon Group Bravo)
1 Apr 1966 Phu Bai - 1st Force Reconnaissance Company OP CON 3rd MAF
27 Apr 1966 Phu Bai - Company A (-) 1st & 2nd Plt , HDQTS Section Op Con 4th Marines
28 Apr 1966 Phu Bai - Company B (Rein) Recon Group Bravo
13 May 1966 OP CON Recon Group Bravo Chopped to 1st Force Reconnaissance Company
14 May 1966 Danang Company A (-) returned to OP CON 3rd Recon Battalion,
Phu Bai - 3rd Plt Company A OP CON 1st Force
1 Jun 1966 Danang Company A (-), C, H&S
Phu Bai - Company B Rein
23 Jun 1966 Dong Ha -1st Force Reconnaissance Company (-) displaced to Dong HA, 1st Plt Company A
30 Jun 1966 Danang - Company A (-), C, D(-) H&S
1st Force & Company B OP CON 4th Marines
12 July 1966 Dong Ha - Company A HDQTS & 2nd Plt OP CON 4th Marine Regiment (Hastings)
15 Jul 1966 Dong Ha - 5th Plt 3rd Force Recon Company OP CON 1st Force
20 Jul 1966 Danang - Company A, HDQTS, 1st, 2nd Plt OP CON 3rd Recon Bn -
31 Aug1966 Danang - Company A, C, D, H&S Danang
Phu Bai - Company B
June 23 to 21 Nov 1966 Dong HA - 1st Force Reconnaissance Battalion –Rein
8 Sep - 2 Dec 1966 Dong HA
2 Dec 2nd Plt Company A chopped OP CON 4th Marines Dong Ha. 6 December 66 - 2nd Plt to SLF 1/4
10 Oct 1966 Phu Bai - Command Group 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion moved to Hue Phu Bai
31 Oct 1966 Danang - Company A, C, D, H&S –
Phu Bai - 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion, Company B, H&S, and Det. 3rd Force Reconnaissance Company. Det H&S Company
30 Nov 1966 Phu Bai - 3rd Recon Bn (-), H&S, A, B, Det 3rd Force -2 plt’s
Dong Ha – 3rd Recon Bn (-) , Det H&S Co., C, D
31 Dec 1966 Phu Bai - 3rd Recon Bn (-), H&S, A, B, Det 3rd Force -2 plt’s
Dong Ha – 3rd Recon Bn (-) , Det H&S Co., C, D
Khe Sanh – 3rd Force 1 plt.
31 Mar 1967 Phu Bai - 3rd Recon Bn (-), H&S, A, B, Det 3rd Force -2 plt’s
Dong Ha – 3rd Recon Bn (-) , Det H&S Co., C, D
Khe Sanh – A Co. (-), 3rd Force 1 plt.
30 Apr 1967 Phu Bai - 3rd Recon Bn (-),H&S, B, 3rd Force Recon Co
Dong Ha – 3rd Recon Bn (-) , Det H&S Co., C, D
Khe Sanh – A Co
31 May 1967 Phu Bai – H&S, B, C
Dong Ha – D, 3rd Force Recon
Khe Sanh – A (Rein)
30 Jun 1967 Phu Bai – H&S, A, C
Dong Ha – D, 3rd Force Recon
Khe Sanh - B

Dong Ha – C, 3rd Force Recon
Khe Sanh - B

Dong Ha – D, 3rd Force Recon

Dong Ha - 3rd Force Recon

Dong Ha - 3rd Force Recon
Cam Lo - A

13 Aug 1969 Company C redeployed to Okinawa
7 Oct 1969 Company A redeployed to Camp Pendleton, CA
3rd Force OP Con III MAF – deployed to Phu Bai
19 Nov 1969 Company E deactivated
22 Nov 1969 Company H&S, B, D redeployed Okinawa
