Donald Bannister Gallery

Donald Bannister Photo Gallery

The photo's contained in this gallery are from the collection of Donald Bannister and have been made available to all by his wife Beau. Donald passed away on 24 June 1996. Beau wanted us to have them to share. The photo's were selected from a large collection and represent the time frame between Summer 1966 to Spring 1967. They are men from Alpha Company 2nd Platoon [Hoovers Hunters] 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion.
