About this site

This site continues the Alpha Recon Association website built and kept by George G. Neville Jr. His pages have been reorganised by subject and joined up, so that a name, a date or a patrol leads to everything written about it. His words, names and dates are as he wrote them.

Where the site adds something of its own, such as a section description or a note that an album is no longer online, it says so or is plainly a label. Pages that had been lost from the original site and were brought back from the Internet Archive say so under their title.

Alpha Company was one company of the 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion. The battalion's own association, covering all of its companies, is the 3rd Recon Association (3rdrecon.org). The Camp Reasoner album shown here comes from its website.

How names are linked

A name is linked directly to a man's page only when first name, middle initial and last name all match one man on the roster. Anything less certain is offered as a possible match, for the reader to judge. See all possible matches.
