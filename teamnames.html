Alpha Company Team Names/Call Signs

3rd Reconnaissance Battalion

Reconnaissance Teams used a particular call sign to designate a team during radio communications. These call signs became team names. The name may have been passed on during the years and sometimes used by different recon companies. This page will be used to record as many names as can be remembered. If you know a call sign used by Alpha not listed here please notify George Neville so the name can be added to the listing. They are presented alphabetically.
