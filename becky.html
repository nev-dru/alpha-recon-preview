Honor and remembrance

John D. Killen, III

In Memory

John D. Killen

30 June 1967

Team Striker

"HIS DUTY"

FIGHTING FOR OUR COUNTRY
IS A RIGHT THEY SAY
MANY PEOPLE AGREE
BUT THERE IS ALWAYS A PRICE TO PAY
WHETHER IT BE LIFE, LIMB OR MINDS
FIGHTING FOR OUR COUNTRY
IS A LOSS OF ALL KINDS

I SOMETIMES THINK
WE ARE NO BETTER THAN THEM
WHEN WE RAISE OUR LITTLE BOYS
TO FIGHT LIKE BIG MEN
I KNOW WE FIGHT TO KEEP
OUR COUNTRY SAFE
BUT A BROTHER LIKE MINE
CAN NEVER BE REPLACED

YOU SEE HE FOUGHT FOR ALL
THE RIGHT REASONS
BECAUSE THAT
IS WHAT HE WAS TAUGHT
BUT I WILL ALWAYS MISS
MY BROTHER "JOHN"
I LOVED HIM A LOT

I LOVE MY COUNTRY
AS YOU CAN SEE
I CONDONED A DEATH
SO YOU AND I CAN BE FREE

Rebecca Killen-Bailey

Alpha Recon Association. Alpha Company, 3rd Reconnaissance Battalion, United States Marine Corps, Vietnam 1965 to 1969.
